## Platform

## Stack

Astro (static output), deployed to https://erptek.net. English only.

## Users

Primary: owners and CEOs of small-to-mid-sized companies. They know AI is changing how work gets done and feel pressure to act, but they don't know where to start, which tools to trust, or whether their company's data and processes are in any state to use AI. They are evaluating whether ERPTEK is a credible partner to call.

## Product Purpose

ERPTEK is a startup consultancy and build partner that makes companies ready for AI and then puts AI to work for their employees. Four connected offers:

1. **Second brain**: a company knowledge base that captures documents, decisions, procedures and know-how so people (and agents) can find and use them.
2. **Agent assistants**: AI assistants built for specific roles and workflows that help employees do their daily work better.
3. **Advisory**: assessment of the company's current situation (people, process, systems, data) and a practical roadmap.
4. **AI readiness / migration prep**: cleaning, structuring and connecting data, and mapping workflows so they are ready for AI.

Success: a qualified owner books a discovery call / readiness assessment.

## Positioning

Starts from the company's actual situation and data, not from a tool. The name signals roots in ERP and business systems: ERPTEK treats the company's operational data and workflows as the foundation, builds the second brain on top of it, and only then deploys agents. Readiness first, agents second.

## Operating Context

Owners evaluate on a laptop between meetings or on a phone after a referral. They compare against generic AI agencies and software vendors. They need to understand the offer in plain business language, see a clear process, and know what the first step costs them in time.

## Product Principles

1. Readiness before automation: data and workflow come first.
2. Speak to owners in outcomes (time, clarity, fewer errors), not models and tooling.
3. Show the method, because there is no track record to show yet.
4. Every page leads to one low-risk first step: the readiness assessment / discovery call.
5. Honest claims only.
